RadarAuditor measures how visible the website of a business is on Google, Bing, Apple Maps, ChatGPT and Claude, and what is missing.

For roofers, dentists, kitchen studios and every business whose customers come from nearby, and for agencies and consultants who offer audits to their own clients. The first audit runs from the outside and needs no access to your accounts.

How an audit runs

You enter the website address and start the audit. An artificial intelligence runs it from start to finish: it calls every measurement service, looks at every response, scores every check and writes the evaluation and the action plan. Programs only do handiwork that needs no judgement, such as printing the PDF and sending the phone notification.

  1. App on the phoneYou enter the website address, pick the checks and start the audit. The app reads business, address and phone number from the legal notice of the website. Progress appears live in the app.
  2. Cost estimateThe app reads the website, up to 60 pages, calculates the cost estimate and makes one trial request to every paid data service; if the balance with the data services does not cover it, the audit does not start and a message names the missing amount.
  3. MeasurementThen all measurements run, at the same time wherever they do not wait for each other, ordered by provider: Google (with AI Overviews, AI Mode and Gemini), Bing and Copilot, Apple Maps, ChatGPT, Claude and paid visibility (Google Ads, Meta Ads). Every measurement uses the provider's official interface first, otherwise a paid data service, the browser last; every fallback route is logged with its reason in the measurement log. If a route fails, the check reads “not measured” with the reason, and the audit goes on.
  4. ScoringEach of the 62 checks is passed, partly passed, failed or not measured. Passed counts 1, partly passed 0.5, failed 0; checks that were not measured or were deselected do not count. Checks without a proven threshold, such as the share of AI mentions and the map grid, show the measured value and the comparison with the competitors instead of a verdict.
  5. ControlA separate control step gives every check three verdicts with evidence from the unchanged responses: data collected correctly, data correct, evaluation correct. A tick without evidence counts as a cross; a cross takes the check out of the overall score and the provider scores. In the app every check shows a progress bar and behind it three boxes, with the reason in plain language for a cross.
  6. Evaluation and action plan as PDFFrom the stored, controlled measurements two PDF files are built: the evaluation and the action plan, one measure for every check with three ticks, sorted by impact. The cost statement of the audit and a phone notification with a link to the evaluation come with them. In the app everything can be walked through with the customer.

Five providers, one number

The score is a weighted average of five providers; the weights add up to 100. Checks, weights and thresholds live in a single check catalogue with 62 checks, 38 of them criteria with the provider's source. 11 checks only state a fact and do not count towards the score, among them all six of paid visibility with Google Ads and Meta Ads.

What is measured, and what is not

A lot can be measured from the outside, but not everything. What could not be measured appears in the report as “not measured” with the reason and does not count towards the points, instead of being guessed.

Measurable from the outside, no access needed

  • Whether Google, Bing, the ChatGPT search and Claude may read the website: blocks in robots.txt, error pages, blocking notes in the page code and firewalls
  • Page titles, headings, image descriptions, readable links, the sitemap and whether every page is reachable under one address only
  • Load time on the phone
  • Whether Google and Bing see the same page as visitors
  • Google profile: main category, description, services, opening hours, photos, attributes, number and average of reviews compared with the businesses on the map
  • Positions in Google and Bing search, on the map and in image search; listing in Apple Maps and on Bing
  • Whether Google's AI answer, ChatGPT and Claude name the business, and the map grid around the location: as a measured value compared with the competitors, without a verdict, because there is no proven threshold for it

Only with customer access or paid data

  • Customer access: calls, routes and views from the Google profile, search words with real clicks and the figures from the Google Ads and Meta ad accounts are only available with access to the business's accounts.
  • Links from other sites, search volume, competitors’ search terms, ads on Google, Facebook and Instagram: only with a paid data service; once the set limit is reached, these checks read “not measured”.
  • Profile verified, the notification service IndexNow, how current the pages are: not always certain from the outside; then the check reads “not measured” with the reason, never a guess.
  • Development over time: needs a second run and is not measured in the first audit.
  • A measurement route fails: the affected checks appear with the reason in the result, and the audit goes on.
  • Two queries of the same search can turn out differently; the report therefore names the time of every query.

Finding and looking after customers

For providers who sell audits with the app: the app finds businesses that would benefit from an audit, gives undecided customers real reply drafts to take away and prepares the work for every month of a subscription. What is promised is this work, never a particular position on Google.

  1. Finding customersIn the “Kunden” (customers) tab you enter a trade and a town, for example roofers in Münster. The app reads up to 60 businesses from Google Maps and sorts them by gaps in their Google profile, each with one sentence saying why: no website, no opening hours, no phone number, few photos or clearly fewer reviews than half of the list.
  2. Add or audit right away“Ins CRM als Interessent” (add to the CRM as a prospect) creates the business in the customer relationship management system (CRM) with an open case. Only what you copy from the public Google profile is stored. “Audit starten” (start audit) checks the business's website just like the “Prüfen” (audit) tab.
  3. Only permitted ways of contactEach business shows the ways that section 7 of the German Act against Unfair Competition allows: letter and personal visit, a phone call only with a concrete reason, an advertising email without consent never. That is why there is neither an email nor a dial button. Anyone who has objected to advertising is on a block list and appears without any way of contact.
  4. Costs and storage time cappedThe customer search has its own daily limit and a monthly limit in euros, so the audits never run short. After 24 hours the list is deleted automatically.
  5. Reply drafts as a PDFWhen a customer says “let me think about it”, the app writes up to five replies to their real reviews that have no reply yet and presents them as a PDF. Nothing is published on Google.
  6. Monthly serviceOnce the first optimisation is finished, drafts are prepared after every monthly audit of a subscription: replies to new reviews and suggestions for looking after the profile, such as opening hours, photos, posts and description, never a change to the website. You approve each draft on its own.

Operation and data protection

RadarAuditor runs on its own secured server at Hostinger in the European Union. Your data is protected. The service complies with the General Data Protection Regulation and with the artificial intelligence regulation of the European Union (EU AI Act).

Server and protection of data

  • Own secured server: The app and its data are held on a server of its own at Hostinger in the European Union.
  • Data protected: Access rules apply to every table of the database.
  • No passwords: The remote audit needs no access to your accounts. For ongoing care you invite us to your Google profile; your password is not needed for that, and you can withdraw the access at any time.
  • Only what the audit needs: What is stored is the details of the business, the search terms, the measured values with the unchanged responses of the providers, and the reports.
  • Time-limited lists: The list from the customer search is deleted automatically after 24 hours.

General Data Protection Regulation and AI regulation

  • General Data Protection Regulation: The service complies with the General Data Protection Regulation. Anyone who has objected to advertising is on a block list and is not approached again; the app offers neither advertising emails without consent nor phone calls without a reason.
  • Artificial intelligence regulation of the European Union (EU AI Act): The service complies with the regulation. The texts of the evaluation and the action plan are produced with artificial intelligence and are labelled as such.
  • Control with evidence: A separate control step checks every measurement against the unchanged responses; a tick without evidence counts as a cross.
  • Approval by a person: Every change to the website or the Google profile arrives as a draft and is only carried out after your approval.
